== Why Intel Gaudi for AI Inference?

* **Purpose-built for AI**: Intel Gaudi 3 is designed from the ground up for deep learning training and inference workloads
* **High throughput**: 128 GB HBM2e per card with 3.7 TB/s bandwidth delivers consistent performance under load
* **Scalable**: Tensor parallelism and expert parallelism across multiple cards
* **Production validated**: This lab has been load-tested at 150 concurrent users with zero errors and sub-second tool calling
* **Enterprise-ready**: Integrated with Red Hat OpenShift AI for deployment, observability, and lifecycle management
